How we work

No mysticism, no performance

Plenty of yoga online is either spiritual in a way that puts people off, or athletic in a way that hurts beginners. Both lose the people who'd benefit most.

Zenviaaa is plain: short sessions, clear instructions, props whenever they help, and a coach who watches your form. We're not medical professionals — for pain or injury, see a doctor or physical therapist, and we'll work around what they tell you.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I'm extremely inflexible.

That's the starting point for most students here, and flexibility is the result rather than the requirement. Props exist precisely for this.

I have a bad back or knee.

Get your doctor's or physical therapist's guidance first. Once you have it, we build the practice around their restrictions — we don't diagnose or treat.

Do I need equipment?

A mat helps, a wall is useful, and two books work as blocks. We sell nothing.

Is this spiritual?

Not in our classes. We use ordinary language for the poses and the breathing. If you want the philosophy, there are teachers who do it well — that isn't us.

I can only manage ten minutes.

Then ten minutes is the program. Consistency at ten minutes beats an hour you skip.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want to try one class?

Thirty minutes, free, at whatever level you're at. Nobody watches but the coach.
