The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, all built on short daily sessions and form checked on video.

Program 01 · Start from zero

Beginner foundations

Most beginners quit because a class moved too fast and something hurt. We work on a small number of poses, using props without apology, with a coach watching your video to catch the alignment problems that cause pain later.

  • 8 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • 20 min a day
  • A mat, or carpet

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Standing

Feet, knees and hips, without collapsing into the joints.

Weeks 3–4 · The floor

Seated and lying poses, modified for tight hips.

Weeks 5–6 · Linking

Moving between poses with the breath.

Weeks 7–8 · A full practice

Twenty minutes you can do on your own.

Weeks 1–2 · Hips

The tightest area for most desk workers, opened gradually.

Weeks 3–4 · Upper back

Extension and rotation to counter rounding forward.

Weeks 5–6 · Your routine

A personal ten-minute sequence you'll keep.

Program 02 · Undo the chair

Mobility for desk workers

Sitting for eight hours shortens some things and switches others off. We build short, targeted routines you can do at your desk or beside it, plus two longer sessions a week. If you have pain rather than stiffness, see a physician or physical therapist first — we'll work alongside their advice.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 10 min a day
  • No equipment

Start this program

Program 03 · More than stretching

Strength & balance

Yoga builds real strength when poses are held with intent, and balance is a use-it-or-lose-it skill. This program adds controlled holds, single-leg work and core stability — useful whatever your age, and particularly worth doing before you think you need it.

  • 10 weeks
  • After foundations
  • 25 min a day
  • A mat and a wall

Start this program

Weeks 1–3 · Holds

Building time in the poses you already know.

Weeks 4–6 · Balance

Single-leg work, with a wall nearby at first.

Weeks 7–8 · Core

Stability that supports the back rather than crunching it.

Weeks 9–10 · Flow

Putting it together in a sequence that challenges you.

Weeks 1–2 · The anchor

Attaching practice to something already in your day.

Weeks 3–4 · The minimum

A five-minute version that still counts.

Weeks 5–6 · Disruption

Travel, illness and busy weeks, planned for.

Weeks 7–8 · On your own

Your own sequences, without following along.

Program 04 · Past week three

A practice that sticks

Almost everyone can do yoga for two weeks. This program is about the rest of it: tying practice to something you already do daily, keeping a five-minute version for terrible days, and restarting without guilt when life interrupts.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 10–20 min a day
  • Nothing new

Start this program
